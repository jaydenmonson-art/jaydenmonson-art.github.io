Jurassic Jungle: Shoe Room Story Video

A video for the shoe-room monitor, so guests understand the story before they enter the playground.

Overview

Jurassic Jungle is a kids’ playground with a story throughout. Kids wear interactive wristbands to log their progress as they find missing hatchlings and return them to their nest. I owned the touchpoint in the shoe room, before guests enter.

Role

I designed the touchpoint and made the motion and video in After Effects.

Challenge

I needed guests to understand the story and the instructions before they entered the chaotic playground. Signage was already there. Early on, very few people stopped to read it, and guests said they didn’t know what was going on at first. I made a video for the monitor to increase dwell time and understanding.

Process

  1. 01

    I observed guests and logged what I saw.

  2. 02

    I planned the key messages to deliver.

  3. 03

    I storyboarded the video.

  4. 04

    I filmed and gathered footage.

  5. 05

    I built it in After Effects over several iterations, tightening the visuals and using animation to direct attention.

  6. 06

    I installed the video on the TV.

  7. 07

    I observed again.

  8. 08

    I fixed the volume and the gap between loops, and I shipped those edits for day 2.

Video

A clip from the shoe-room story video: playground footage with red lava-style lighting.

Outcome

After I installed the video, I saw more dwell time and more attention from both kids and parents. That read is qualitative. I did not record counts.
