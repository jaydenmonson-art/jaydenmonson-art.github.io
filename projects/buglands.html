Concept Design / Theming · Butterfly Biosphere, Thanksgiving Point

Buglands

A concept to retheme the Costa Rica Climber as Buglands, a playground of bug habitats.

Overview

Concept work to retheme the Costa Rica Climber in Thanksgiving Point’s Butterfly Biosphere. The play structure was not really working, and there was not much theming. Jayden walked the climber and photographed it, then developed Buglands: regions themed to different kinds of bugs. He built a concept deck for the whole idea and modeled a few touchpoints.

Role

Concept designer. Site research, the theme and story, the concept deck, and initial 3D touchpoint models.

Challenge

Retheme the existing Costa Rica Climber. It was not really working as a play structure, and the space had little theming.

Regions

  • Colony Cliffs

    Bug communities: ants, termites, and social wasps. In the deck this is the entrance, where colony builders make tunnels, chambers, and towers.

  • Hopper Plains

    The toddler area. Grassland and grasshoppers: soft fields for bouncing, leaping, and playing from a grasshopper’s view.

  • Wetland Wonders

    Water habitat: diving beetles, water striders, whirligigs, and scorpions in arid riverbeds. The deck also calls this Bug Basin.

  • Bug Borough

    Underground or forest-floor bugs. Tunnels, bioluminescence, glowworms, isopods, beetles, roaches, and decomposers.

  • Canopy Crossing

    Above, in the rainforest canopy: tropical insects, stick bugs, and assassin bugs. The deck splits the canopy into Build a Bug, Find a Bug, Fly a Bug, and the Butterfly Passage.
