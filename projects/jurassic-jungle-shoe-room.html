Jurassic Jungle: Shoe Room Story Video

A video for the shoe-room monitor, so guests understand the story before they enter the playground.

Overview

Jurassic Jungle is a kids’ playground with a story throughout. Kids wear interactive wristbands to log their progress as they find missing hatchlings and return them to their nest. Jayden owned the touchpoint in the shoe room, before guests enter.

Role

Designer and motion/video creator for the touchpoint (After Effects).

Challenge

Help guests understand the story and the instructions before they enter the chaotic playground. Signage was already there. Early on, very few people stopped to read it, and guests were saying they didn’t know what was going on at first. The task was a video for the monitor, to increase dwell time and understanding.

Process

  1. 01

    Observations, logging insights in a document.

  2. 02

    Planned the key messages to deliver.

  3. 03

    Storyboarded the video.

  4. 04

    Filmed and generated placeholder footage.

  5. 05

    Built it in After Effects over several iterations, optimizing the visuals to minimize confusion and using animation to direct attention.

  6. 06

    Installed the video on the TV.

  7. 07

    Observed again.

  8. 08

    Fixed the issues that showed up — volume, and the time between loops — and shipped edits for day 2.

Video

A clip from the shoe-room story video: playground footage with red lava-style lighting.

Outcome

After the video was installed, he observed more dwell time and more attention from both kids and parents. That read is qualitative. No counts were recorded.
